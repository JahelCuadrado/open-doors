# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

First version for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric): play your singleplayer world with your friends over
the internet.

- Open your world from the vanilla Friends List ("My world": Closed, Open to friends or Invited
  only), separate from LAN.
- A green dot on the Friends button when a friend's world is open to you; they show first in the
  Friends List with a Join button.
- Invite friends from their row, even with your world closed; invites arrive as a toast, join from
  the Friends List or press `K` in game.
- See who is playing, kick or block players.
- Real Minecraft accounts and encryption, direct connections (no relay yet), automatic router
  port mapping (UPnP, NAT-PMP, PCP) and IPv6.
